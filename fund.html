Built so the structure protects you

These are rules of the system, not promises.

The manager cannot withdraw your money

Money leaves the fund account only through redemptions and trading results. Ordinary withdrawals and transfers are blocked.

One NAV for everyone

Gains and losses are shared by units. Every holder, the manager included, is priced at the same NAV.

Fees only on new profit

The performance fee applies above your high‑water mark. A recovery from a loss is not charged twice.

Fair entry and exit

Joins and redemptions happen only when the fund has no open positions, so nobody enters or leaves on unrealised numbers.
