STEP01

Open your account

Accounts are created on the website — not inside the apps. Go to alpexa-sports.com and choose Sign up.

  1. Enter your email and choose a password.
  2. Confirm your email address from the message we send you.
  3. You receive an account number. Keep it — you can sign in with either the account number or your email.
Three accounts, one login

Your registration opens a Sports, a Crypto, and an FX account under the same login. They hold separate balances, and you move money between them yourself — instantly and with no fee. This matters: money arrives on Crypto, and betting happens on Sports.

STEP02

Sign in — the right screen finds you

On the sign-in page, enter your account number or email, your password, and pick the server you want: Sports, Crypto, or FX. You don't choose a layout — we detect your device and open the interface built for it.

Install it like an app

On a computer, your browser offers an Install option in the address bar — that puts ALPEXA on your desktop and opens it full-screen with no browser chrome. On a phone, use Add to Home Screen from the browser's share menu.

Sessions are per tab

Each browser tab keeps its own session. A brand-new tab starts signed out — refreshing the tab you're in keeps you signed in. Closing the browser signs you out.

STEP03

Add money — on the Crypto side

All funding happens on your Crypto account, whether you're on the phone app or the desktop dashboard. Sign in with Crypto selected, then open Deposit.

Crypto → Wallet → Deposit

Buy with a card Fastest

Pay with Visa, Mastercard or Apple Pay through our payment provider. The provider verifies your ID once, the first time only. Your USDT lands in your ALPEXA wallet.

Send USDT you already own No provider

Copy your deposit address from the Deposit screen and send USDT to it from your own wallet or exchange. Credited once the transfer confirms on-chain.

Read before you send

USDT on Ethereum (ERC-20) only.

We support exactly one network for on-chain deposits. USDT sent on Tron (TRC-20), BSC, Polygon or any other chain — and any coin that isn't USDT — cannot be recovered by anyone, including us. Your exchange will ask you to pick a network on the withdrawal screen. Pick Ethereum.

✓ USDT · ERC-20 (Ethereum) USDT · TRC-20 (Tron) Other chains

Always copy the address from the Deposit screen inside the app, right before you send. Never reuse an address from a screenshot, an email, or a chat message — that is how people get robbed.

Sending between ALPEXA members

To receive coins from another ALPEXA user, use the Internal tab and share your @handle instead of an address. Internal transfers move on our books — any coin, no network to choose, nothing to lose.

STEP04

Move it to Sports

Your deposit lands on Crypto. Betting spends your Sports balance, so move what you want to play with across.

Wallet → Transfer → from Crypto to Sports

Transfers between your own accounts are instant and free. Move funds back to Crypto whenever you like — you don't need to transfer anything before withdrawing.

STEP05

Place a bet

Sign in with Sports selected. Browse Today or In Play, and tap any price to add it to your Bet Slip. Markets shown are Spread, Total and Moneyline; soccer is priced Home / Draw / Away.

Three ways to combine

Singles
Each selection settles on its own.
Parlay
Every leg must win. Odds multiply, so the payout climbs fast — and one loss ends the ticket.
Same Game Parlay
Several markets from one game. Because those outcomes are related, the combined price is reduced compared with a normal parlay. The slip shows the payout you'll actually get before you confirm.
Why some games can't be bet

A game only opens when we hold a real price from the market. If our odds provider hasn't published that game yet, it appears locked — you'll see the fixture but no prices. We never invent a number to fill the gap. Prices are also suspended for a moment right after a goal or a score change.

Following your bets

Open My Bets for Open and Settled tickets. When a game finishes, settlement is automatic — winnings are credited to your Sports balance without you doing anything.

STEP06

Withdraw

Withdrawals are paid in USDT on Ethereum (ERC-20) to a wallet address you provide.

Crypto → Wallet → Withdraw
  1. Enter the amount and paste your USDT (ERC-20) receiving address — it starts with 0x and is 42 characters long. Or scan the QR code from your wallet.
  2. Press Request Withdrawal. The amount leaves your balance immediately and is held against the request.
  3. The request goes to our back office; you'll see it as Pending until it's sent. If it's rejected, the funds are returned to your balance.
Three things worth knowing

You don't need to transfer first. Withdrawals are routed out through your Crypto account automatically, wherever the money currently sits.

A flat network fee applies. It's deducted from the amount you request — the Withdraw screen shows the fee and the exact net amount you'll receive before you confirm.

Bonus credit isn't cash. If your balance includes a welcome bonus or locked funds, those can't be withdrawn or transferred. The Withdraw screen shows your Withdrawable figure — that is what you can take out.

Double-check the receiving address before you confirm. As with deposits, an on-chain transfer to a wrong or non-Ethereum address cannot be reversed.

TOOLS07

Staying in control

These live under Settings in the Sports app. They are yours to set — we don't need to approve them.

Daily deposit limit
Caps how much you can add in a day. Set it to 0 to remove the limit.
Daily loss limit
Stops new bets once your losses for the day reach the amount you set. Set it to 0 to remove the limit.
Reality check
A reminder on a timer you choose, showing how long you've been playing.
Self-exclusion
Locks betting and deposits for a period you pick. It cannot be shortened or lifted early — choose the length deliberately.
